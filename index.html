Built in the open

Work I maintain in public, where the code is the whole argument.

qredential

MIT · published on npm · zero runtime dependencies

Verifies a digital credential carried in a QR code with no network connection. Selective disclosure and key binding per RFC 9901, revocation checked from a cached list, in TypeScript on WebCrypto alone.

It comes straight from the eCNH above. The hardest part of that project was never the app, it was the roadside: an officer scanning a licence with one bar of signal, needing a yes or no in under a second. The libraries for this are enterprise SDKs, heavy and tied to one country's profile. This is the version a product engineer adds on a Tuesday.

Documented in nine languages, conforming to WCAG 2.2 AA, with a playground that runs the library in your browser and fires ten real attacks at the verifier so you can check its claims instead of trusting them.
