Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Quishield and Token Bank are offline apps developed by George Veras Valentim. They do not collect, store on external servers, transmit, sell or share any personal data. They contain no analytics, no advertising and no third party tracking SDKs. All information you enter or scan stays only on your device and is removed when you delete the app.

Quishield

Quishield uses the camera solely to read QR codes; images are processed in memory on the device and are not saved or sent anywhere. Scan results and history are stored only on your device and can be cleared at any time inside the app.

Token Bank

Token Bank stores the profiles, tokens and rewards you create only on your device. Nothing is transmitted, synced or backed up to external servers by the app.
